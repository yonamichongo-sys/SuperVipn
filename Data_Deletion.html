Data Deletion

Delete Your Data — Super ViPN

Last Updated: October 2026

Super ViPN ("the App") does not require an account, username, or password, so there is no traditional "account deletion" step. Instead, the App's technical connection data — the information needed to operate your VPN tunnel — is deleted either automatically or on request, as described below.

How to request deletion

Choose any one of the following methods:

  1. Automatic — Disconnect in the App. Open Super ViPN and tap Disconnect. This immediately and permanently removes your device's connection data from our servers — no waiting period, no further action needed.
  2. Automatic — Uninstall the App. Uninstalling Super ViPN removes all data stored locally on your device (your encryption keys and session preferences). If you did not tap Disconnect first, your device's server-side connection record is automatically deleted after a short period of inactivity (see the retention table below).
  3. Manual request — Email us. If you want your connection data deleted immediately and cannot open the App to disconnect (e.g. you already uninstalled it), email us at greybirdproject@gmail.com from the email address you used (if any) or include the approximate date and time you last used the App. We will locate and delete the associated record and confirm by reply within a reasonable time.

What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

Data Deleted / Retained
WireGuard public key & assigned virtual IP address Deleted immediately when you disconnect in the App. If never manually disconnected, it is automatically deleted after 30 days of inactivity, or after 7 days if the device registered but never actually connected.
WireGuard private key Never sent to our servers. Stored only on your device, encrypted via Android KeyStore. Deleted the moment you uninstall the App.
Local session preferences (timer, selected server) Stored only on your device. Deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it.
IP address used for rate-limiting abuse prevention Held in server memory only, never written to disk, and automatically discarded within 1 hour.
Advertising ID (via Google AdMob) Not collected or stored by us directly. Managed by Google; you can reset or delete it at any time via your device's Settings → Google → Ads menu.
