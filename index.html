Privacy Policy for Super ViPN

Effective Date: October 2, 2026
Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Super ViPN ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how the Super ViPN Android application ("the App") handles information when you use our Virtual Private Network (VPN) service.

1. Strict Zero-Log Policy (Browsing & Network Traffic)

Super ViPN operates as a strict no-logs VPN service. When you connect to our WireGuard VPN tunnel, we do not monitor, record, log, store, inspect, or sell:

  • Your browsing history, visited websites, or DNS queries.
  • The contents, payload, or destination of your network traffic.
  • Your original (ISP-assigned) IP address after disconnection.
  • Connection timestamps or per-user bandwidth histories linked to your identity.

2. Use of Android VpnService

Super ViPN uses the official Android android.net.VpnService API as its core functionality to establish an encrypted device-level WireGuard tunnel between your Android device and our VPN server infrastructure.

  • All network traffic routed through the VPN tunnel is encrypted on your device using modern WireGuard cryptography before transmission.
  • The VPN tunnel is only active when you explicitly accept the in-app disclosure and tap the Connect button.

3. Ephemeral Technical Data Processed for VPN Connectivity

To provision and release your WireGuard tunnel session, the App processes only minimal, non-identifying technical parameters:

  • WireGuard Public Key: Your device generates a local Curve25519 keypair stored securely using Android KeyStore encryption. The private key never leaves your device. Only the public key is transmitted over HTTPS to our VPN provisioning server (/register) to allocate a temporary virtual IP address, and to /deregister when you disconnect so your peer session is removed from the server.
  • Local Session Preferences: Your remaining session timer and selected server node are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Abuse Prevention (Rate Limiting): To prevent automated abuse of our registration system, our server temporarily checks the IP address a registration request comes from against a short-term limit. This check happens in memory only, is never written to persistent storage, and is automatically discarded after one hour.

4. Third-Party Services Used by the App

Super ViPN integrates the following official Google SDKs to provide free ad-supported access and protect server infrastructure from automated abuse:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): We display banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded video ads (which allow you to add +30 minutes of free VPN session time). Google Mobile Ads may collect and process your device's Advertising ID, coarse location/IP information, and ad interaction diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Integrity API: Used during VPN peer registration to verify that connection requests originate from an authentic, untampered Android device and app binary.

6. Data Security

All communication with our provisioning API uses HTTPS/TLS encryption, and all VPN tunnel traffic is protected with WireGuard encryption (ChaCha20-Poly1305 / Curve25519). Your WireGuard private key is encrypted at rest on your device using Android KeyStore AES-256-GCM.
